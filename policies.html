Elkhoi

Policies & Terms

Information about Elkhoi’s service terms, customer responsibilities, payments, cancellations and privacy practices.

Privacy Policy

We collect information such as your name, phone/WhatsApp number, location, service requirements and other details you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to enquiries, arrange services, confirm bookings and quotations, communicate with customers and coordinate service providers where necessary. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information and do not sell customer personal information. You may contact Elkhoi with questions or requests concerning your personal information.

General Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to Cleaning, Laundry, Maintenance and Property Management services. A submitted enquiry is not automatically a confirmed booking. Final prices depend on the actual requirements, condition, location and agreed scope. Additional work, materials, parts or specialist services may be charged separately where applicable and will be communicated before proceeding where reasonably possible. Customers should provide accurate information and reasonable access required for the service. Service concerns should be raised with Elkhoi as soon as reasonably possible. Elkhoi may decline or postpone work where conditions are unsafe or outside the agreed scope.

Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Cancellation or rescheduling of a confirmed booking or appointment must be made at least one day before the scheduled service date, or on the scheduled service date before 08:30. Requests made after 08:30 on the scheduled service date may be treated as a late cancellation or missed appointment. If Elkhoi needs to reschedule, we will communicate with the customer and arrange another suitable time. Changes to the agreed service may result in a change to the price or scope, with the customer’s agreement.

Cleaning Policy

Cleaning bookings are confirmed after Elkhoi confirms availability, scope and the agreed price. Once our cleaning team arrives and confirms the agreed scope of work, 50% of the agreed service fee is payable before cleaning begins. The remaining 50% is payable immediately upon completion. If the property condition or requirements differ materially from what was described, any additional work or price adjustment will be discussed before proceeding. Customers should contact Elkhoi as early as possible if they need to cancel or reschedule.

Laundry Policy

The minimum laundry order is 3 kg. Pickup and delivery follow Elkhoi’s scheduled routes, and bookings should be made at least one day before pickup. At pickup, 50% of the laundry charge plus the full pickup/delivery fee is payable; the remaining 50% is payable at delivery. Express/same-day service, where available, carries a +50% charge. Final weight and price are confirmed by Elkhoi. Customers should identify delicate items and remove valuables from pockets. Elkhoi takes reasonable care, but results may vary depending on fabric, stains, existing damage and care requirements.

Maintenance Policy

Elkhoi manages your maintenance needs from request to completion, using trusted and vetted technical professionals where required. Maintenance work may require an assessment or quotation before work begins. Parts, materials or specialist services may be charged separately. If additional work is discovered, Elkhoi will communicate the additional requirement and cost before proceeding where reasonably possible. Customers must provide reasonable access to the property and relevant areas. Larger projects may require a separate quotation and agreed project arrangements.

Property Management Policy

Property Management services are provided according to the specific scope agreed with the customer. Services may include property checks, inspections, upkeep coordination, cleaning coordination, maintenance coordination and reporting. An inspection does not automatically include repairs or cleaning unless these are included in the agreed service. Customers are responsible for providing necessary property information and access arrangements. Additional services or third-party work will be discussed and agreed separately where applicable.

Photo & Media Consent

Where appropriate, Elkhoi may take photographs of completed work for service records. Use of customer or property photographs for marketing purposes is optional. Customers may decline marketing consent without affecting their ability to request or receive Elkhoi services. Elkhoi will not intentionally publish a customer’s name, contact details or other identifying information with marketing material without separate consent.
