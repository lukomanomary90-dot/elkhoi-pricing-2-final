Cleaning Payment Terms

Once our cleaning team arrives and confirms the agreed scope of work, 50% of the agreed service fee is payable before cleaning begins. The remaining 50% is payable immediately upon completion of the agreed cleaning service.

If the actual property differs materially from what was described, any price adjustment must be agreed with the customer before additional work begins.

Final pricing may vary because of property condition, location, number of bathrooms, additional rooms/areas, heavy dirt, heavy grease, heavy mould, excessive rubbish/debris and additional requested services.
